# Object desk

Homepage and chrome rebuilt from `main` around the reference still-lifes: a chartreuse floppy on black, a grainy cream paper scan, a navy cassette with orange transport, a red cartridge, and a scrapbook of dots and clips.

The profile picture is the original JPEG at `public/images/avatar.jpg`, unmodified. It sits in the floppy label. Page paper is the cream of those scans (`#f3efe4`); dark mode is studio black (`#0e0e0e`).

Motifs are CSS and small SVG. They do not reproduce album art, character drawings, or console wordmarks from the references.

Essay bodies are unchanged. The writing index is presented as tracks.
