Imagine a world with two doors. The instruction says that the blue door leads outside. Halfway through the task, the agent finds a fresh note: the blue door is now locked.

The interesting part begins when the old plan stops working.

## Change one thing

This fictional experiment changes a single fact in an otherwise fixed environment. We want to watch what the agent does with the new evidence.

| Step | Observation | Question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Blue door is the exit | Does the agent make a plan? |
| 2 | A new note says it is locked | Does the agent notice the contradiction? |
| 3 | Another route is available | Does the agent revise its plan? |
