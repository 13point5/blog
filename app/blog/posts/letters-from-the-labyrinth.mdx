Reinforcement learning is a labyrinth that redraws its walls while you walk.

You pick a reward, you release an agent, and soon the agent finds a corridor you never intended — a shortcut through the rubric, a loophole in the environment, a beautifully wrong policy that scores well and means little.

## A cartographer's checklist

- Keep a **golden skeleton**: environment + simple reward before cleverness.
- Inspect **rollouts** the way a naturalist inspects specimens — in batches, with a copy button for further prompting.
- Write **tests for the rubric** before you trust the curve.
- Assume **reward hacking** is the default, not the exception.

Vibe RL is not vibes instead of science. It is agents in the loop with the same tools a researcher would hold: CLIs, dashboards, datasets, and the discipline to prove each turn of the maze.

The compass in the center of the stamp is optional. The habit of looking again is not.
