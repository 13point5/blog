# Two ways to browse — fifth iteration

Branch: `explore/color-studio`; draft PR #18. Original base: `main` at `6baab35`.

## Current direction

Explore organizing structures for a mixed physical-media collection. The previous scattered desk and random shuffle have been removed. A small Library / Record store navigation lets the owner compare two complete experiences:

- `/`: a media library. Articles are grouped into Learning machines (experiments, reinforcement learning, world models) and Understanding machines (architecture, evaluation, formal verification). The objects rest on shared pale metal shelves. Each shelf scrolls horizontally on a phone, with scroll snapping and keyboard support; all articles remain normal links.
- `/browse`: a record-store bin. Choose a thematic collection, then flip through a stack. The selected object comes forward beside its title, summary, and reading link. Previous/next buttons, Left/Right/Home/End keys, and horizontal touch swipes browse the stack. A swipe changes the selected piece without navigating; tapping a reading link opens its full page. There is no auto-advance.

The avatar, compact header, neutral surface, indigo links, and individual object colors remain. Shelf objects lift on hover/focus; the bin animates changes in depth. All animation respects reduced-motion preferences. Article and About routes remain conventional full pages.

## Content

The original Vibe RL article is intact. Five sourced draft explainers cover reward design and RL, evaluation, Transformer architecture, proof checking, and world models. They remain labeled as drafts and excluded from indexing and the sitemap. Each has an original SVG diagram, generated by `scripts/draw-note-figures.py`, with descriptive alt text and references. No fabricated research results or new experiments are attributed to the owner.
